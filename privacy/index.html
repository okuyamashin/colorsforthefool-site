Privacy policy

4 October 2026

Colors for the Fool (https://colorsforthefool.engawa5656.com/) is operated by Engawa Inc. This page describes the information we handle when you look at the site and when you send an inquiry.

Inquiries

The inquiry form receives a name, an email address, and the message. No login is required.

We use these only to reply to the inquiry. We do not use them for advertising, and we do not sell them. We store them in an Amazon Web Services database, and the operator reads them from an administration screen. We keep them as the record of that matter until the reply is finished, and for as long as the record is needed.

What you draw

The story you are reading stays only in that tab. It disappears when you close the tab.

Cookies this site sets

The list of cards you have drawn, the design you chose, and your choice about measurement stay in cookies in the browser. Each time you open a page, the browser sends those cookies to the server. The server does not read them, and it does not keep a record of them.

Measuring visits

To see how often pages are opened, we use Google Analytics. Through cookies and similar means, Google receives the page address and a broad picture of how the site is used. This measurement is for improving the site, and we do not connect it with the contents of inquiries. For how Google handles this, see Google's privacy policy.

Visitors from the EEA (the EU, plus Iceland, Liechtenstein, and Norway), the United Kingdom, and Switzerland are asked before measurement on every page. The wording is in the language of the page they have open. Google Analytics does not run until they accept, and it stays off if they refuse. When the country cannot be determined, and for other countries, measurement starts when the page opens. The Cookies link in the footer opens the choice again.

Who we entrust it to

We use Amazon Web Services to deliver the site and to store inquiries. Inquiry records are kept in the Tokyo region. We use Google to measure visits. The information that measurement sends is handled by Google in the United States. Information about people in the EU may also be handled outside the EU. Each company handles the information only within the scope its role requires.

Disclosure and deletion

To ask for disclosure or deletion of your own inquiry, email info@colorsforthefool.engawa5656.com. We respond after confirming that the request is from you.

Changes

When we change what this page says, we publish the new text on this page with a new date.
