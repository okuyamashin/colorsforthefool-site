Colors for the Fool

Design

    Editorial fashion

    Black velvet sleeves fall to the edge of the card. A gold corset counts each breath, and behind dark glasses only the gaze remains. The chain becomes jewelry and circles the throat. The two who kneel split left and right, like a magazine spread. Perfume comes back through the paper and stops at the throat. A fingertip turns the page, and a cold ring arrives first.
