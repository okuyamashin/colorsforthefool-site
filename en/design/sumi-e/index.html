Colors for the Fool

Design

    Ink painting

    The ink keeps flowing after the brush has been set down. Horns are spatters. The robe is one wide stroke. The chain becomes links at the end of the ink that drips. A pine is small on the right. The moon is broken. Two red seals are pressed into the blank. The reading of the seals has blurred since they were pressed, and is about to disappear. So much is left white that nothing seems to have been written. In the center of that white, the shape of your knees is already wet.
