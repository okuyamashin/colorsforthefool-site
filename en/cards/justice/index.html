    JUSTICE

    XI

    Upright

    Justice is the meeting point of decision and discernment, guided by both the sword and the scales. It asks for more than detachment: it calls for the courage to look at every side equally, to measure with sincerity and clarity. Justice is not about cold logic, but about holding yourself to the same honest standard you would expect from others.

    This card invites you to set aside personal preference and shine an even light on every perspective involved. It is about weighing actions and consequences fairly, letting each side receive the same consideration. Acting justly means seeing where you might favor your own interests, and consciously balancing that impulse.

    Justice urges you to be thorough and truthful, not only with others but with yourself. It is the process of restoring balance—not through punishment, but by aligning deed and result with integrity. Today, it reminds you to be as fair to others as you wish them to be to you.

    Reversed

    Reversed, Justice warns of imbalance or self-justification. The scales are tilted, yet you may convince yourself they are not, shaping facts to suit a decision already made. Sometimes, the desire for fairness becomes indecision, and the sword—meant for clarity—remains unused.

    Accountability can shift onto others, while explanations and excuses fill the space between what you did and what happened next. Instead of honest reflection, you might count only what benefits your own side, avoiding uncomfortable truths about your actions and their impact.

    This card asks you to look honestly at what you place on your side of the scale. Are you measuring with real fairness, or hiding from responsibility? Reversed Justice is a call to confront bias and restore true balance, starting with your own choices and words.
