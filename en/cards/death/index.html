    DEATH

    XIII

    Upright

    Death arrives as the silent harbinger of completion. Fields stand harvested, and the flag faces the dawn, signaling not destruction, but the gentle clearing of what has already run its course. In this space, what cannot remain in its old shape must be set aside, so that what is truly needed can take root in the new season ahead.

    There is wisdom in recognizing what may be left behind and what must be released, even if only in form. Like stems of last season returning to the soil, endings make way for fresh beginnings to emerge. What once filled a place now creates room for the next life, the next chapter, to grow.

    Today, this card embodies the act of naming what is finished and allowing empty space to welcome what comes next. It is a quiet acceptance of change, and the understanding that new growth requires the gentle surrender of what has passed.

    • Black #000000 There is solace in endings that clear the way for new outlines.
    • White #FFFFFF Quiet rituals clear the way for new outlines.
    • Snow #FFFAFA The world is hushed for renewal.
    • Pink #FFC0CB May what ends bring the gentlest beginning.
    • Gold #FFD700 Endings make space for the next beginnings.
    • Orange #FFA500 The new outline awaits, bright and tender.
    • DarkOrange #FF8C00 A threshold, a fruit’s final gift, a warmth that outlasts the night.
    • Ivory #FFFFF0 Trace the edge of something old today, and sense the outline of what comes next.
    • FloralWhite #FFFAF0 An ending, woven with flowers and morning, is the outline of all that comes next.
    • GhostWhite #F8F8FF Endings have a shape; beginnings wait quietly at their edge.

    Reversed

    Despite the approach of an ending, effort is spent maintaining the familiar shape. There is a reluctance to acknowledge the season's close, trying instead to explain away the withered branches as though their time has not come. Change is put aside, stored as something to be organized later, while the reality of transformation waits quietly.

    When Death is reversed, the ground intended for renewal appears only as a heap of remains. What cannot be let go becomes clutter, crowding the space where something new might have been placed. The refusal to clear out what has finished blocks the path for growth and reinvention.

    Today, this card stands for the moment of choosing to release a role, a habit, or a name that has fulfilled its purpose. It asks for a decision to let something end, so that the shelf may finally hold what is next, rather than what lingers past its time.
