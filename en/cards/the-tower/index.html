    THE TOWER

    XVI

    Upright

    A tall structure, once steadfast, is struck by sudden force, exposing its vulnerabilities. The tower represents certainties built too high, foundations that have grown rigid from comfort or pride. When the crown topples and the sealed windows shatter, the truth of the ground below becomes unavoidable—a humbling honesty that cannot be ignored.

    Not everything is lost in the collapse. What falls are the layers and defenses that separated you from the world, the illusions that kept you elevated and isolated. The ground remains, and in its simplicity, there is something real to stand on once the dust settles.

    This card asks you to open a window in your own structure, to let in fresh air before the storm breaks it down for you. The shock of change is as much invitation as it is destruction, bringing clarity to what was hidden behind closed doors.

    • FireBrick #B22222 Begin with what endures, and build again.
    • Crimson #DC143C When what is inside must come forth, do not turn away.
    • Black #000000 The ground beneath holds all that falls, and all that will rise.
    • SlateGray #708090 Endure the weather; let what was hidden breathe.

    Reversed

    After the lightning, there is a temptation to rebuild the tower exactly as it was, restoring every stone to its previous, precarious height. Alternatively, fear of future collapse may drive you to seal every crack, painting over necessary faults to maintain the illusion of security.

    When the tower stands reversed, change is stalled and pressure builds within. Parts meant to fall remain stubbornly in place, supporting a roof that should already have come down. The weight of the past lingers, and the refusal to release it keeps you trapped in a tense, unyielding space.

    This card suggests it is time to acknowledge what has already fallen, to recognize the rubble without rushing to restore or hide it. Acceptance of loss, even in small pieces, makes room for honest renewal and gentle recovery.

    • Khaki #F0E68C The walls still stand, even as the air whispers collapse.
    • Tan #D2B48C Wear what endures through weathering.
    • AntiqueWhite #FAEBD7 Let the old forms hold a little longer.
