    Tæflstānas

    Blæc and hwīt flōr geedlæcþ þā ilcan fēowerecge, swā hwider þū stepst. Se tæflstān on middan hæfþ hornas and fiþeru. Þā lǣssan stānas on ǣgþre healfe habbaþ gāta hēafdu, and sind mid racenteage tō þǣm stæle gesewen. Se heap behindan him ne styrede. Nis nān ūtgang of þǣm borde þǣr man genumen stān lecgan mæge. Þū setst finger for þon þe hit is þīn tīd, and þæt ān fēowerecge forlīest þīnes stānes blēo.
