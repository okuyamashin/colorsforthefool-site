Se mōna is þynne on þǣre uppan swīþran. Se burg is feorr. Wīnleaf crēopaþ andlang þæs ymbhwyrftes. Þæt hwīte betwēonan þām līnum is papir þe gȳt ne āsniþen is. Rīm þā līnan āne and āne, and on middan yrnþ ān of him forþ ofer þīne hȳd, on wege þe gȳt ne drȳge is.
